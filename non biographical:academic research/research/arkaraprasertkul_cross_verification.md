## Conflict Zones

### Conflict C1: h-index comparison across disciplines (RESOLVED)
- **Conflict**: h-index 12 is +71% above architecture mean but -33% below social sciences associate professor range (17-35)
- **Resolution**: The discrepancy is entirely attributable to disciplinary citation culture differences, not research quality. Architecture and anthropology are among the lowest-citation disciplines. When normalized for field, both metrics are consistent.
- **Status**: RESOLVED

### Conflict C2: SJR values for Journal of Urban Design (MINOR)
- **Conflict**: Dim02 reports SJR 0.642 from Scimago, but one source (journalimpact.org) reported SJR 0.806
- **Resolution**: Temporal variation in SJR values. The 0.642 figure is from the most recent Scimago data and is the authoritative source.
- **Status**: RESOLVED
