Non Arkaraprasertkul publishes across a **diverse portfolio** spanning elite Q1 urban studies journals, mid-tier specialty journals, and selective book chapters. ## 7. Critical Assessment

### Strengths
1. **Elite anchor publication**: The Urban Studies paper (2018) is a major achievement — this is a top-5 global urban studies journal
2. **Strong Q1 concentration**: 57% of journal articles in Q1 venues
3. **Cross-disciplinary breadth**: Publications span urban studies, heritage, anthropology, planning history, and urban design
4. **Improving trajectory**: Venue quality has improved over time, culminating in Urban Studies
5. **Heritage studies presence**: IJHS is a leading venue in heritage studies

### Weaknesses
1. **One exceptional paper, then a drop-off**: After Urban Studies (SJR 1.98), the next highest is IJHS (SJR 0.802) — a significant gap
2. **Low-tier early work**: Footprint (Q4) and Harvard Asia Quarterly (non-indexed) are weak venues
3. **Journal of Planning History (Q3)**: Below the Q1 threshold, though understandable for a historical planning piece
4. **Book chapters**: Not indexed in Scopus/Web of Science, limiting citation tracking
5. **No publications in Nature Sustainability, Landscape and Urban Planning, Cities, or Habitat International** — the highest-impact urban journals

### Overall Assessment

Arkaraprasertkul's journal portfolio demonstrates **solid above-average quality** with one exceptional publication (Urban Studies). The weighted average SJR of **0.943** is respectable and comparable to established urban scholars. The improving trajectory over time is a positive indicator of growing scholarly recognition and ability to place work in increasingly selective venues. However, the portfolio lacks depth at the very top tier — only one paper in an SJR>1.5 journal — and includes three lower-tier venues (Q3, Q4, non-indexed).
