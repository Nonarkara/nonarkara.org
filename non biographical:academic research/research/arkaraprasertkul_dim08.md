# Dimension 08 — Citation Context & Audience Analysis

## Non Arkaraprasertkul — WHO is Citing His Work and in WHAT Contexts

---

## Executive Summary

Non Arkaraprasertkul's work is cited across a remarkably broad interdisciplinary and geographic landscape. His scholarship attracts citations from **urban studies, anthropology, heritage studies, urban geography, planning history, architecture, sociology, and cultural policy**. The geographic spread of citing authors spans **East Asia (China, Japan, South Korea), Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Thailand), Europe (UK, Spain, Italy, Germany, Netherlands), North America (US, Canada), and Australia**. His key conceptual contributions — particularly "gentrification from within," "gentrification and its contentment," and "gentrifying heritage" — are not merely referenced in passing but are frequently engaged as **central theoretical frameworks** around which citing authors structure their arguments. His work appears in **major handbooks, edited volumes, systematic reviews, and dissertations**, signaling emerging canonical status within the gentrification-in-China literature. However, **direct policy citations and syllabus inclusions remain limited**, suggesting the audience is primarily academic rather than practitioner-oriented.
