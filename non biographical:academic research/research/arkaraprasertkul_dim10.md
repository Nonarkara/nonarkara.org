## 4. The "My City" App: Flagship Innovation Project

The **"My City" app** in Nakhon Si Thammarat represents Arkaraprasertkul's most significant practical contribution during this period [^407^][^435^][^440^].

**Project details:**
- **Origin:** Deployed as a crowdsourced reporting tool for flood-prone waterways in Nakhon Si Thammarat
- **Mechanism:** Citizens report clogged waterways via mobile app; data combined with analytics to identify root causes
- **Scale:** 44,000 users (40% of municipality population)
- **Impact:** Saved **10 million baht (~$275,000 USD)** in operational costs [^435^]
- **Expansion:** Scaled to multiple municipalities across Thailand; DEPA aims to reach **7,000+ municipalities by 2027** [^407^]
- **Platform:** Built on Line (the dominant messaging app in Thailand) rather than a standalone app — a key design decision for adoption

> "In Thailand, many people follow Line Official Account because they get notified about new information. This is more useful than creating a standalone app that needs to be downloaded" — Dr. Non Arkaraprasertkul [^435^]

**Confidence: HIGH** — Multiple sources including Kyoto Review, GovInsider, and DEPA's own website.
